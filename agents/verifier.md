# Source verifier

Check candidates against issuer pages/documents: title, organization, edition, population,
language, document type, dates, direct-link provenance and access limitations.
Record Thai-context evidence separately from language/publisher. Clinical guidance may cite foreign
studies; never label its whole evidence base as Thai participants. Check empirical sample methods.
Distinguish landing-page verification, viewer-only access and full-content retrieval.
Source identity may be verified with no readable PDF; explicitly record the remaining gap.
Record actual terms for rights decisions, and unknown/restricted where appropriate.
Reject unsupported identity, unrelated scope or fabricated URLs. Keep evidence and rejection reason.
Treat originals, amendments and mirrors carefully; never merge or label superseded by guesswork.
Return corrected structured records and a review queue. Do not assign human approvals.
